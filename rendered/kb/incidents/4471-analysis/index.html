Parser: day and month reversed

The shared parser reads dates month first even when the tenant writes day first. In the export’s rule, this covers app and email reports, plus form reports that do not use the misleading booking-v3 hint. For example, R-41200 describes 7 April becoming 4 July. R-41205 belongs here despite its intake tag of hint: its tenant is morrow.tax, locale fr-FR, form booking-v2, and its channel is app.

Expected fix: parse according to the tenant’s explicit date contract, or pass an unambiguous machine date. Verify both ambiguous and unambiguous dates across the affected channels. The export supplies classification logic, not production parser code; these describe the expected behavior rather than a test result for the reported fix.

Hint: the form tells people the wrong format

For a day-first tenant on booking-v3, the form prints MM/DD/YYYY, but reads the input in the tenant’s order. Following the hint therefore changes the date. This is a separate form contract problem, even if the visible symptom resembles the parser issue. R-41230 is a copper.studio form report and belongs here despite its intake tag of parser.

If the form were retained, the displayed format and its interpretation would need to agree with the tenant’s locale. The actual course outcome is retirement of booking-v3, so this issue is not planned for correction. Changing only the shared parser would not establish that this form problem was fixed.

Proposed triage rule and next actions

The accepted 2024 decision and the triage page recommend merging by subject. Their text is contradicted by the executable examples: identical subjects can have different causes, and different subjects can share one cause. Their review dates are old; accepted is not evidence that the recommendation still matches the system.

Keep that accepted decision intact. For the desk’s review, propose a new decision record that groups by verified cause after checking tenant locale, form version and channel. An unknown tenant remains unplaced until investigated. Matching a cause groups reports for an engineering issue; it does not prove two different report IDs are the same submission. Deduplicate the export by report ID first.

Track the parser and hint separately and close them with the distinct reasons in the engineering update. Keep the one-day-move notes visible, and verify any new live reports against the tenant directory before merging them. Rebuild this page when the data changes: its counts are computed, while the issue bodies are dated snapshots and any revision must be reviewed.

The support desk can keep receiving reports after this export. Do not treat a known status, an intake tag, or a historical merge as proof that every report has the same cause. A closed issue may mean fixed or not planned; always preserve its closure reason.
